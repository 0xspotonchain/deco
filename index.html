{sniper}

Sniper

A testnet-only buy/sell sniper for the burner wallet on Arc, Robinhood, LitVM and Velana. Dry-run by default — live mode needs explicit approval, every time. Pools, name service and volume mode are modeled on our friend 0xzunid's approach — credit where it's due.

Pair watcher (polling — public RPCs expose no mempool), target-token mode for DECO, TP / SL / time exits, and spend caps per trade and per run. Live trading needs LIVE.enabled plus --live; either one alone keeps it in dry-run.

Status: LIVE — volume mode running. First run complete on 3 chains: 1,000/1,000 rounds each on Arc, Robinhood and LitVM (6,007 txs). Velana is at 522/819 rounds and still running. From Tue Nov 17 the sniper runs 100 rounds/chain/day at 11:11 GMT+7.

Uniswap v4: traded live. The position-mode sniper did 2 full buy→sell cycles on the real Robinhood v4 DECO/ETH pool (4 txs confirmed, total net cost 0.0000125 ETH, burner DECO exactly unchanged). First cycle: buy ↗ / sell ↗. Second cycle: buy ↗ / sell ↗. Two v4 gotchas fixed along the way: the docs-listed Quoter isn't a Quoter on this testnet (manual StateView quote instead), and the router reverts on empty hookData (swaps encode 0x00).
